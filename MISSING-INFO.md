# GNDEC Compass: details to collect on campus

This list covers information that was not available in a sufficiently clear, current and verifiable public online source during the project research. The map and photos currently in the app are useful orientation aids, but they do not replace an on-site check.

## Best next information to collect

1. **Admissions cell:** exact building, floor, room/counter number, entrance landmark, current visiting hours, phone/email, and a clear exterior/interior photo. The public sources reviewed did not establish a photo-guided route to the admissions counter.
2. **Room-by-room routes:** confirm which building and floor every room code belongs to, then record paths from the nearest gate, stairs/lift, corridor turns, and destination door. Current room-to-block matches are inferred from room codes and timetable data.
3. **Accessible paths:** step-free routes, ramps and lift locations, accessible washrooms, steep/blocked paths, and which gates remain open. No verified accessible route graph was found.
4. **Current campus paths and distances:** walk the proposed paths and record distance, crossings, barriers and time. The app currently uses map geometry and estimated walking distance; it is labelled approximate.
5. **Admission and student-service destinations:** exact locations and current hours for the academic branch offices, examination branch, accounts, scholarship/help desk, placement cell, medical room, security office and hostel offices.
6. **Transport:** current college bus routes, stop names, departure times, route contact and service-day changes. Public information reviewed did not provide a complete, maintained route dataset.
7. **Photo set with permission and context:** current, well-lit photos of the admissions counter, each academic block entrance, named classroom/lab doors, library entrances and floors, auditorium entrance, workshop entrance, gates and major junctions. Include photo date, direction faced, building/floor/room label, source/photographer and permission to reuse.
8. **Official update contact:** a college office or student group that can confirm timetable changes, closed routes and moved offices, plus how often those details should be checked.

## Already located online

- [GNDEC official site](https://gndec.ac.in/) and [official photo gallery](https://gndec.ac.in/?q=node%2F69): campus and event photos; the gallery does not consistently identify the exact photo location or capture date.
- [GNDEC official timetables](https://appsc.gndec.ac.in/time_tables): section, subgroup, teacher and room timetable exports. The data included in this build points to the 17 September 2026 export; confirm the latest version before relying on a room/time.
- [GNDEC library information](https://library.gndec.ac.in/?q=node%2F17): library location and floor information.
- [GNDEC auditorium facilities](https://gndec.ac.in/cultural/?q=facilities): auditorium information and photos.
- [Shared college campus map](https://www.gndpoly.org/CollegeMap2.php): useful for shared GNDEC/GND Polytechnic landmarks, but not a verified indoor navigation map.
- [OpenStreetMap copyright and attribution](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright): source attribution for public map geometry.

## Please verify before adding

- Exact office and classroom locations, current hours, route safety/accessibility, live bus information, and image reuse permission.
- Whether shared-campus facilities have GNDEC-only access rules or hours.
- Any named student record or lookup feature. Student rosters and student search indexes are intentionally excluded from this public repository; use a consented, official and access-controlled source if that feature is ever needed.
