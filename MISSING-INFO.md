# GNDEC Compass: real-world data still to collect

This list covers information that was **not available in a sufficiently clear, current and verifiable public source** during the build. The app labels these gaps instead of inventing data. An on-site check is the next step.

## Best next information to collect

1. **Admissions cell** — the 2026 public admission poster says the admission office is at **Training & Placement, Seminar Hall, ground floor, PG Block**, with `admission.gndec.ac.in` and public enquiry numbers. The app’s map pin is still an unverified Administration Block placeholder, so confirm the exact counter, entrance, visiting hours, phone/email and take clear exterior/interior photos before publishing a route.
2. **Room-by-room routes** — confirm which building and floor every timetable room code belongs to, then record paths from the nearest gate, stairs/lift, corridor turns and destination door. Current block/floor values are inferred from room-code patterns (F/G/S) and timetable names, not independently surveyed.
3. **Accessible paths** — step-free routes, ramps, lift locations, accessible washrooms, steep/blocked paths and which gates stay open. No verified accessible-route graph was found.
4. **Current campus paths and distances** — walk the proposed routes and record distance, crossings, barriers, lighting and time. The app currently estimates from OSM geometry and labels the result approximate.
5. **Student-service destinations** — exact locations and current hours for branch offices, the examination branch, accounts, scholarship/help desk, placement cell, medical room, security office and hostel offices.
6. **Transport** — current college bus routes, stop names, departure times, route contacts and service-day changes. No complete maintained public dataset was found.
7. **Photo set with permission and context** — current, well-lit photos of the admissions counter, every academic-block entrance, named classroom/lab doors, library entrances and floors, auditorium/workshop entrances, gates and major junctions. Record photo date, direction faced, building/floor/room label, photographer and reuse permission.
8. **Official update contact** — a college office or student group that can confirm timetable changes, closed routes and moved offices, plus how often to re-check.
9. **Complete timetables for later years** — the current official export is complete for first year but partial for some 2nd/3rd-year groups. Collect verified section/subgroup timetables, room changes and elective groupings before presenting later-year schedules as complete.
10. **Verified notes library** — moderated, permission-cleared notes per subject/semester. The app currently says no verified notes library exists.
11. **Official exam date sheets and result links** — full semester date sheets, seating plans and official result portals. Current data has exam notices only, not complete date sheets.
12. **Mentor and office contact policy** — written permission and a clear privacy policy before publishing any mentor phone number, staff cabin number or student contact detail. The current public roster intentionally omits private contact fields.

## Already located online

- [GNDEC official site](https://gndec.ac.in/) and [official photo gallery](https://gndec.ac.in/?q=node%2F69): campus/event photos, though locations and capture dates are not consistently identified.
- [GNDEC official timetables](https://appsc.gndec.ac.in/time_tables): section, subgroup, teacher and room exports. This build points to the **17 September 2026** export; confirm the latest version.
- [GNDEC library information](https://library.gndec.ac.in/?q=node%2F17): library location/floor information.
- [GNDEC auditorium facilities](https://gndec.ac.in/cultural/?q=facilities): auditorium information and photos.
- [OpenStreetMap copyright and attribution](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright): public map geometry attribution.

## Please verify before adding

- Exact office/classroom locations, current hours, route safety/accessibility, live bus information and image-reuse permission.
- Whether shared-campus facilities have GNDEC-only access rules or hours.
- Any new student record or lookup feature. Use a consented, official and access-controlled source; do not add private contact data to this public static build.
- That the old GNDEC Compass project and Worker remain untouched; this project links to them but does not deploy over them.
